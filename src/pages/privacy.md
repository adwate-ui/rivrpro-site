## What we collect

Only what you enter in the enquiry form.

## What we do with it

It reaches us as an email, sent through our email provider, Resend. We use it to reply to you, and for nothing else. We don't store it elsewhere, add you to a mailing list, or share it.

## Spam checks and analytics

Cloudflare Turnstile checks that the form comes from a person; Cloudflare sees your IP address to do this. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits without cookies. The site sets no cookies of its own.

## How long we keep it

Up to 24 months after we last speak. Ask us to delete it at any time.

## Your rights

Reply to our email or message us on WhatsApp to see, correct or delete what we hold.
